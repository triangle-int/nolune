## Your space, our space

A profile is a space for a person or a group, rather than a separate login. One account can belong
to several profiles, and you can switch between them whenever you need to.

| Space              | Who it's for           | What you might keep there                         |
| ------------------ | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Your own profile   | Just you               | Personal projects, ideas, and preferences         |
| The family profile | Everyone at home       | Meal plans, household notes, and family routines  |
| A shared project   | The people taking part | A holiday, a birthday, or something you're making |

Each profile has its own conversations, memory, skills and assistant personality. In a shared
profile, everyone can pick up the same conversation, and the assistant knows who's speaking.

## Bringing someone into the family

1. Give them an account. As an admin, open **People** from the menu under your name, then either:
   - **Create invite link** and send it to them. They open it, pick their name, email and
     password, and they're in. A link works once, for 7 days, and you can take it back.
   - Or **Add a person** with their name and email, and send them the address and the password
     nolune shows you.

   At the terminal, `nolune user invite Anna` prints an invite link, and
   `nolune user create Anna anna@example.com` makes the account and prints a password.

2. Open **People & profile** in the profile you want to share, and add them by name or email.

They can create their own profiles and add existing users too. Only admins make accounts; there's
no sign-up page, and joining a profile doesn't send an email invitation.

**People** is also where an admin resets someone's forgotten password, makes someone an admin, or
removes an account.

## Your name and picture

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and open **Settings** to change the name everyone
sees, or to add a picture: pick one, drag it into the circle, zoom, and save. Messages you already
sent keep the name you had then. Your picture shows next to your name in shared chats and under
**People & profile**.

## A new profile's welcome

A new profile starts with a short welcome: pick the assistant's avatar, and bring over what
ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini already knows about you (copy a prompt there, paste the answer back). On a
fresh install without a model, an admin picks one there too. Its sounds can be turned off in
Settings.

## The assistant's look

There's one assistant; each profile gives it a look of its own. Pick one in the welcome, under
**People & profile**, or with `nolune profile avatar <name> --profile <slug>`.

## Soul

Each profile can tell nolune who to be for its family: its character, what it cares about, how it
talks. Write it under **People & profile**, or just ask nolune to be different and it updates its
own soul (`~/.nolune/profiles/<profile>/soul.md`). Every chat starts with it.
