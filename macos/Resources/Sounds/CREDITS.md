# The app's sounds

The macOS app's own sounds; the song and `confirm.mp3` are the web welcome's
(`packages/web/src/lib/assets/sounds/welcome`), which `scripts/build-app.sh` copies in too.

| File       | Plays for                                             | Source                                                                            | License |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| `boom.mp3` | the big bang, heard far away a moment after it's seen | [Big Boom](https://freesound.org/people/unfa/sounds/189779/) by unfa on Freesound | CC0     |

`boom.mp3` is made distant from the original: a softer attack, a little saturation so laptop
speakers hear its low end, low-passed at 420 Hz, a long dark reverb heard more than the boom
itself, and faded out by 9 seconds. CC0 asks for no credit; it is kept here to know where it came
from.
