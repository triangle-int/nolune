Use the pictures as the identity reference: keep every person's face recognizable, and change only their outfits and hairstyles to {{theme}}. Outfits are modest; poses are varied, silly and PG.
A photo-booth collage: three black-and-white vertical strips side by side and misaligned, four photos in each strip, each strip slightly tilted so they overlap, in a tall vertical composition.
In some photos people are partly or completely out of frame, switch places or lean in close to the camera.
Harsh direct flash, extreme contrast, crushed blacks, blown-out whites, few midtones and lost detail. Coarse film grain, overexposure, thick black borders and very light wear, like an analog booth print.
